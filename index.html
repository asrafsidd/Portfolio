Who I Am

About Me

I'm a mechanical and robotics engineer with strong expertise in mechanical design, simulation, and experimental validation of robotic and electromechanical systems. I'm proficient in CAD-driven product development, nonlinear FEA, multiphysics simulation, and physics-informed modeling - with hands-on experience in embedded hardware prototyping, sensor integration, and additive manufacturing.

Currently a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at the University of Notre Dame's miNiRo Lab, I lead research-driven hardware projects across underwater robotics, soft robotics, and energy systems - from concept through testing and deployment. My work has been funded by the Department of Defense and published in venues including IEEE RA-L and ICRA.
